How fast can you arrange a pickup? +

For FTL loads in the Chicago metro, often within 4–6 hours. Regional and cross-country pickups typically book within 24 hours. Expedited and hot-shot freight can be dispatched same day.

Are your carriers insured and vetted? +

Every carrier in our network is verified for active DOT authority, cargo insurance, and safety rating before their first load. We re-check compliance monthly and drop carriers with any lapses.

Do you handle customs for cross-border freight? +

Yes. We work with licensed customs brokers on both the Canadian and Mexican borders, prepare commercial invoices, and coordinate bonded carriers so your freight clears without delays.

What about claims and damage? +

All shipments carry standard cargo insurance. If damage occurs, file a claim within 15 days and our claims team handles the carrier communication end-to-end — no runaround for you.
