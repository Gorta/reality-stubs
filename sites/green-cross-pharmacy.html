Do you accept my insurance? +

We accept most major insurance plans including Regence, Providence, Moda, UnitedHealthcare, and Medicare Part D. Call us with your plan details and we'll verify coverage before filling anything.

Can I transfer my prescriptions from another pharmacy? +

Yes — and it's easy. Just give us the name of your current pharmacy and the medication list, and we'll handle the transfer for you. Most transfers are complete within a few hours.

Do you offer delivery on refrigerated medications? +

Yes. Insulin and other cold-chain medications are delivered in insulated packaging with temperature monitoring. Delivery windows are confirmed by text on the morning of your delivery.

Can I talk to a pharmacist without an appointment? +

Absolutely. Our pharmacists are available during all opening hours for questions — no appointment needed. For a full medication review, we recommend booking a 20-minute slot so we can give you proper attention.
