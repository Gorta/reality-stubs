Do I need coding experience to start? +

Not for our Beginner courses — over half of our students start with zero experience. If you can use a computer confidently, you can start. We include a free pre-course prep module to get you comfortable with the basics.

Are classes live or self-paced? +

All our courses are live and cohort-based — you learn with a real instructor and classmates, either online or at our Austin campus. Sessions are recorded for review, but the live interaction is where the value is.

What does career support actually include? +

Portfolio and resume review, mock technical interviews, LinkedIn optimisation, and warm intros to our hiring-partner network. Support continues for 12 months after graduation — until you land an offer.

Is there a payment plan or refund policy? +

Yes. We offer monthly payment plans, income-share agreements for eligible students, and a full refund if you withdraw within the first two weeks of your course — no questions asked.
