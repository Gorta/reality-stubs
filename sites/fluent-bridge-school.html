How do I know which level I'm at? +

We start with a free 30-minute placement session — a relaxed chat and a short written task. You'll get a clear level (A1–C2) and a recommended course, with no obligation to enroll.

Are classes online, in person, or both? +

Both. We run in-person groups at our Cambridge studio and live online classes via video call. You can switch between formats week to week if your schedule changes.

What happens if I miss a lesson? +

Every session is recorded (with the group's consent) and you get the materials plus a short catch-up task. You can also join a free make-up session within the same month.

Do you offer a money-back guarantee? +

Yes. If you attend the first two weeks of your course and feel it isn't right for you, we'll refund the remaining balance in full — no awkward questions.
