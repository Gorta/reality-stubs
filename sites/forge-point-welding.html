Certification courses

MIG Welding Fundamentals

Master gas metal arc welding on carbon steel. Includes machine setup, bead technique, joint types, and safety certification.

TIG Welding & Aluminum

Do I need experience to enroll? +

None at all. Our Level 01 courses assume you've never picked up a torch. We start with safety, machine setup, and basic bead technique before moving to joints and positions.

What certifications will I earn? +

Depending on your course, you'll test for AWS D1.1 (structural steel), ASME Section IX (pipe), or both. Exam fees are included in tuition, and you can retake once for free if needed.

Is financial aid available? +

Yes. We accept GI Bill benefits, offer in-house payment plans starting at $180/month, and are approved for several workforce development grants in Texas. Talk to admissions for details.

Do you help with job placement? +

Yes — and it starts before you graduate. We host on-site interviews with partner employers, help with resumes and weld tests, and stay in touch with alumni for two years after certification.
