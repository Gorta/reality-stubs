Ember & Oak is a neighbourhood coffee shop and roastery in the heart of Portland. We source ethically, roast daily, and serve with intention.

Crafted with care, served with warmth

From the farm to your cup — we obsess over every detail so you can simply enjoy the moment.

🌱

Ethically sourced

We partner directly with smallholder farms across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala. Every bean is traceable to its origin.

🔥

Roasted in-house

Our vintage Probat roaster runs daily. Small batches mean fresher coffee and a profile tailored to each origin.

🤝

Community first

More than a café — we host weekly cupping sessions, latte art throwdowns, and local artist showcases.
