About the studio

Behind the lens

“I don't pose people. I wait for the moment when they forget I'm there.”
— Clara Bettencourt, founder

I'm Clara, a photographer based in Lisbon. I picked up my first camera at sixteen and never really put it down. After a decade shooting for magazines and agencies, I opened Lumen & Frame to focus on what I love most: real people, real light, real moments.

My approach is quiet and unhurried. We'll walk, talk, and let the photographs happen naturally. No stiff poses, no fake smiles — just you, at your best, in beautiful light.

I shoot in both digital and 35mm film, and every gallery is edited by hand in my studio in Alfama.

How far in advance should I book? +

For portraits, 2–3 weeks is usually enough. For weddings and events, I recommend 4–6 months, especially for spring and summer dates. A 25% deposit reserves your date.

How many photos will I receive? +

A standard portrait session includes 40–60 fully edited images. Weddings and full-day events typically deliver 400–700 images, all hand-edited and delivered in a private online gallery.

Do you travel outside Lisbon? +

Absolutely. I shoot across Portugal and regularly travel for destination weddings. Travel within 50km of Lisbon is included; beyond that, I'll quote a simple flat travel fee with no surprises.

Can I order prints and albums? +

Yes. I partner with a small fine-art lab in Porto for museum-grade prints, and I offer hand-bound linen albums. Print and album pricing is included in your welcome guide after booking.
