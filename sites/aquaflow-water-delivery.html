What areas of Portland do you deliver to? +

We cover all of Portland proper plus Beaverton, Lake Oswego, Milwaukie, and Vancouver, WA. Enter your ZIP at checkout and we'll confirm instantly.

How much does delivery cost? +

Delivery is free on orders over $35. Below that, it's a flat $4.50 within the metro area. There are no fuel surcharges or hidden fees — ever.

Can I skip or pause a delivery? +

Absolutely. You'll get a reminder text two days before each delivery. Reply "skip" to pause or "change" to adjust your order — no phone call required.

Is the water actually tested? +

Yes. Every batch is tested by an independent lab for minerals, pH, and contaminants. Monthly reports are published on our website and included in every invoice.
