Do I need an appointment, or can I just drop in? +

Walk-ins are welcome for oil changes, batteries, and quick diagnostics. For larger repairs we recommend booking ahead so we can reserve a bay and a loaner car if needed.

What kind of warranty do you offer? +

Every repair is backed by our 3-year / 36,000-mile nationwide warranty on parts and labor. If something isn't right, bring it back — we'll make it right at no cost.

Do you work on European and hybrid vehicles? +

Yes. We service all domestic and import brands including BMW, Mercedes, Audi, VW, Toyota, Honda, and Tesla. Desmond is our certified hybrid and EV technician.

Can I get a quote before you start the work? +

Always. We'll inspect your vehicle, send you a detailed digital estimate with photos, and wait for your approval before turning a single wrench.
