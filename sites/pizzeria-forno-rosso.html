Our Craft

Three rules we never break.

Simple ingredients treated with patience — that's the whole secret.

🌾

48-Hour Dough

We ferment our dough slowly at room temperature for two full days. It develops a deep, nutty flavour and a crust that puffs beautifully without ever feeling heavy.

🔥

485°C Wood Oven

Our hand-built brick oven runs on oak and beech. At 485 degrees, a pizza bakes in ninety seconds — charred at the edge, soft at the centre.

🧀

Morning Mozzarella

Fresh mozzarella arrives from Campania dairies every morning. We tear it by hand, never slice it, so it melts into soft, milky pockets.

Our Story

A family oven, lit every morning since 1987.

Forno Rosso began in a narrow Naples alley, when nonna Elena Rizzo started baking for neighbours who lined up before dawn. Her grandson Marco brought the same recipes — and the same stubborn attention to detail — to this city in 2012.

Today our two ovens still fire up at 6 a.m. The dough is mixed by hand, the tomatoes are crushed by hand, and Marco still tastes every batch of sauce before service.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Yes — we hold tables for parties of two or more. Book up to 30 days ahead by phone or through the form below. Walk-ins are always welcome at the bar counter.

We offer a gluten-free base baked in a separate area of the kitchen. It's prepared on dedicated trays, though our kitchen does handle wheat flour daily.

Friday and Saturday evenings typically run a 20–35 minute wait after 7 p.m. Join the waitlist by phone and we'll text you when your table is ready.

We deliver within a 4 km radius. Neapolitan pizza is best eaten immediately, so we pack each box with a vented lid to keep the crust crisp on the way.
