🎒 Why Summit Path

More than a hiking group

We started Summit Path in 2011 with six friends and a beat-up van. Today we're a 3,800-member club with certified guides, a clubhouse in RiNo, and trips on four continents.

What hasn't changed: we plan every detail so you can focus on the trail, the views, and the people beside you. No competition, no ego — just good company and honest adventure.

  • 🧭
    Certified guides

    Every trip is led by a Wilderness First Responder with local route knowledge.

  • 🎒
    Gear library

    Tents, packs, poles, and snowshoes — borrow for free as a member.

  • 🚐
    Transport sorted

    Van pickups from our RiNo clubhouse on every trip. No carpool chaos.

Do I need to be very fit to join? +

Most of our trips are marked Easy or Moderate and are suitable for anyone comfortable walking 8–12 km a day. Challenging trips require some training, and we'll send a prep plan when you book.

What's included in the trip price? +

Guiding, permits, transport from Denver, lodging or camping fees, and all meals on the trail. Personal gear, travel insurance, and flights (for international trips) are not included.

Is there a membership fee? +

Your first season is free. After that, membership is $60/year and covers clubhouse access, gear library, social nights, and member-only trip discounts.

What's your cancellation policy? +

Full refund up to 30 days before a domestic trip and 60 days before international trips. Within those windows, we'll try to fill your spot and refund if we succeed.
