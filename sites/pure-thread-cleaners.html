Which areas of Seattle do you serve? +

We cover Capitol Hill, Queen Anne, Ballard, Fremont, South Lake Union, Belltown, and Downtown. Enter your ZIP at checkout and we'll confirm your pickup window instantly.

Is pickup and delivery really free? +

Yes — free pickup and delivery on every order over $25. Below that, there's a flat $4 round-trip fee. No fuel surcharges, no minimum-item counts, no surprises.

What if a stain doesn't come out? +

We'll always tell you honestly before we start if a stain is likely permanent. If we can't remove it, you don't pay for the cleaning of that item. No arguments, no fine print.

Do you use eco-friendly cleaning products? +

Yes. We use a biodegradable, petroleum-free solvent system and phosphate-free detergents. Our facility recycles 92% of its water and all of our hangers are reused.
