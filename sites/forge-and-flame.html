Why Forge & Flame

Built on four simple promises

We keep our process slow and our standards high — so you get tools that feel personal from the very first use.

🌿

Recycled materials

Over 80% of our metals come from reclaimed sources. Every piece diverts waste from landfills.

🔥

Hand-fired finish

Each item passes through a 1,200°F kiln, giving it a unique patina no two pieces share.

📦

Plastic-free delivery

Wrapped in recycled kraft paper and shipped in compostable mailers. Zero plastic, ever.

🛡️

Lifetime repair

If it breaks, we fix it. Free repairs for as long as you own the piece — no questions asked.
